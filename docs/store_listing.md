# Koti — Spending & Balance Tracker

## Short Description
Track expenses seamlessly. Beautiful, fast, and completely private.

## Full Description
Koti is a premium personal finance app designed for simplicity and speed. Say goodbye to complex spreadsheets and cluttered accounting tools. Koti focuses on one core question: "How much do I have left?"

**Features:**
- **Lightning Fast Entry**: Add expenses in seconds with our intelligent icon engine that auto-matches your favorite brands.
- **Offline & Private**: Your financial data is extremely sensitive. Koti stores everything locally on your device. No cloud sync, no tracking, no data harvesting.
- **Beautiful Analytics**: Understand your spending at a glance with gorgeous interactive charts and automatically generated insights.
- **Premium Design**: Enjoy a beautifully crafted interface with full Light Mode and Dark Mode support.
- **Total Ownership**: Export and import your data anytime in human-readable JSON format.

Take control of your wealth beautifully.
